\subsection{Refinement of a Lex Specification}
\begin{figure*}
\begin{minipage}{0.48\textwidth}
\begin{lstlisting}
refine epd

refine type person is string

observable event userGivesConsent
    """ person {s} gives consent to receive automated marketing communication from person {r} """
    r: person
    s: person

observable event userWithdrawsConsent
    """ person {s} withdraws consent to receive automated marketing communication from person {r} """
    r: person
    s: person

rule "refine marketing consent"
    whenever
        (NOT userWithdrawsConsent(r, s)) SINCE userGivesConsent(r, s)
    refine
        marketingConsent(r, s)

Figure \ref{fig:rex-article-13} shows one possible refinement
of the Lex specification from Figure \ref{fig:lex-article-13}.
Here the event for prior prior consent gets refined to mean
by two events for giving and withdrawing consent.
Further we might want to be more privacy conscious
and always opt users out of receiving
marketing emails that would be exempt from requireing prior 
consent by the similarity clause in Article 13(2).
This is done by the assumption that every user has objected
to receiving such marketing emails.


\subsection{Pretix}
Pretix is an open source event ticketing system.
It offers products for ticket sales, wait lists,
on location ticket sales, and ticket scanning.
We are interested in the pre-event ticket sales part.
We evaluated a refined Lex formalization of the emaail marketing
regulations in the EU ePrivacy Directive on the Pretix
platform.
