\chapter{Conclusion}
\label{chapter:conclusion}

Motivated by the need for reusable and adaptable legal
specifications in runtime enforcement,
this thesis presents the addition of refinement to Lex.
Refinement in Lex is a flexible and powerful mechanism to
adapt and reuse an existing Lex specification
for different use cases.

In Chapter \ref{chapter:refinement-of-cre-enforcement}
we introduced refinement rules for the CRE[MFOTL] logic.
We showed that this refinement of CRE[MFOTL] conceptually
equivalent to the concept and process of refinement as used
in other areas of formal methods, such as by Sprenger et al.
\cite{igloo} and Abadi and Lamport \cite{refinement-mappings}.
