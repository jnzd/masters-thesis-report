\chapter{Introduction}
\label{chapter:introdcution}

This thesis introduces refinemt of Lex specifications.
Lex is a programming language for formalizing legal specifications,
developed by Hublet et al. \cite{lex} and Degelo \cite{semesterproject}.
Parts of the Lex paper \cite{lex} (in particular those pertaining to refinement)
have been developed in parallel to this thesis.
